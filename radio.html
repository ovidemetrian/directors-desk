Crew Radio

The silent walkie-talkie of Director's Desk. Old crew radios squawk next to the front row. Here the director's instruction arrives as a buzz in your pocket, you answer with one tap, and the director sees who copied.

Your role decides your channel. Reports carry your location, so "can't hear" becomes "Balcony: can't hear". Crew phones can only request HUSH; the director's phone holds the real one, hold to engage. The tablet runs the full desk.

Concept and UX: Ovi Demetrian. Built with Claude. Without a shared show connection, the Radio talks to Desk windows on this same device.
